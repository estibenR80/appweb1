📜 How to Play — Cómo jugar

🇬🇧 English

  1. The board has 20 cards (10 pairs) plus 2 wildcards, all face down.
  2. On your turn, flip two cards.
  3. Match a sentence card with its correct pronoun card.
  4. If they match, you keep the pair and play again.
  5. If not, the cards flip back and it is the other player's turn.
  6. Wildcards: "Extra Turn" lets you flip again; "Lose a Pair" makes you return one pair.
  7. The winner is the player with the most pairs when the board is clear.

🇪🇸 Español

  1. El tablero tiene 20 cartas (10 pares) más 2 comodines, boca abajo.
  2. En tu turno, voltea dos cartas.
  3. Une una carta de oración con su pronombre correcto.
  4. Si coinciden, conservas el par y juegas de nuevo.
  5. Si no, las cartas se voltean y pasa el turno.
  6. Comodines: "Extra Turn" da otro turno; "Lose a Pair" te hace devolver un par.
  7. Gana quien tenga más pares al terminar.

💡 Pronoun Types & Examples

Personal (subject)

I, you, he, she, it, we, they

Do the action → She studies English.

Object

me, you, him, her, it, us, them

Receive the action → I like him.

Possessive

my, your, his, her, our, their / mine, yours, hers, ours

Show ownership → That is mine.

✍️ Examples with Explanations

  • "This is her bag." → The pronoun her shows possession (whose bag it is).
  • "I like him." → The pronoun him is an object pronoun (it receives the action of "like").
  • "That is mine." → The pronoun mine replaces the noun and shows possession without repeating it.
  • "We play together." → The pronoun we is a personal/subject pronoun (it does the action).
  • "Give the book to me." → The pronoun me is an object pronoun after the preposition "to".
